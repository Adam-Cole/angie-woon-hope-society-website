Who we are

The Angie Woon Hope Society

The Angie Woon Hope Society exists to bring hope, comfort, and meaningful support to everyone receiving care through the Blood and Marrow Transplant Hematology Cancer Clinic at the Arthur J.E. Child Comprehensive Cancer Centre in Calgary, Alberta.

Rooted in hope, compassion, and community, we are committed to making a direct impact through advocacy and charitable initiatives. Every dollar raised helps ease the emotional, practical, and financial challenges of treatment and recovery. In honour of Angie, we continue her legacy of kindness and unwavering compassion by bringing hope, comfort, and moments of joy to those who need them most.

About Angie

A life remembered with love

A loving wife, mother, auntie, niece, friend, sister, and daughter, Angie was a lively and compassionate person who always wanted the best for the people she loved. She encouraged others, offered help wherever she could, and tried to make life a little better for her family and friends. She was witty, determined, and had the best laugh.

Angie was diagnosed with Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) in March 2023. It came as a shock to all of us, but in that moment her family and friends came together in a way that showed just how deeply Angie was loved. We supported her through every step, doing everything we could to help. With every hospital stay and every update from the doctors, it was a difficult and heartbreaking battle that Angie fought with courage. Surrounded by her family, Angie took her last breaths on November 27, 2023, at the young age of 43.

Get involved

Help us carry Angie's kindness forward

Founded with a commitment to making a direct impact, the Angie Woon Hope Society ensures that 100% of funds raised directly support individuals and their loved ones by easing the challenges and hardships they face throughout this difficult journey.

Join us in bringing hope to others when they need it most. Whether you choose to donate, partner with us, participate in our fundraising events, contribute a gift in kind, or simply help share our mission, you become part of a community making a meaningful difference. Together, we honour Angie's legacy of compassion and kindness, one act of generosity at a time.
